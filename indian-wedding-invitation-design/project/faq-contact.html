FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know

How long does it take for my invitation link to go live? ▼
Your digital wedding invitation link goes live within 24 hours of submitting your wedding details and muhurat schedule. You receive a personalized link (*shaadiwalacard.com/ananya-kabir*) ready to share on WhatsApp.
Can guests open Google Maps location pins directly from the card? ▼
Yes! Every invitation includes embedded 1-tap Google Maps venue location buttons for all your functions (*Haldi, Sangeet, Shaadi, Reception*). Guests tap once and get turn-by-turn directions directly on iPhone & Android.
Can we collect guest RSVPs and food preferences? ▼
Absolutely. Our suite includes a built-in RSVP form that collects guest headcount, attending functions, dietary choices (Pure Veg, Jain, Non-Veg), and family congratulatory blessings directly to your dashboard.
Is there a limit on how many guests can view our invitation? ▼
Zero limits. You can send your digital invitation link to 50 or 5,000 guests worldwide. It loads instantly on all mobile phones without requiring guests to download any app.
What happens if our venue timing or date changes? ▼
We offer unlimited free edits up to your wedding day! If any muhurat time or venue detail changes, message us on WhatsApp and we update your live link instantly.
